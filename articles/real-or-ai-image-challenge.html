Real or AI? Can You Spot the Fake?

AI-generated images are becoming increasingly realistic. Take this quick visual challenge: one image is a real photograph, while the other was created using artificial intelligence. Can you tell the difference?

Look beyond the first impression

When comparing images, inspect small details rather than relying only on the overall scene. Check whether shadows and reflections match the lighting, whether fine textures look natural, and whether objects connect consistently with their surroundings.

Important: visual clues are not proof

AI image generators are improving quickly, and real photographs can also contain unusual details. A visual clue can help you investigate, but it does not always prove that an image is synthetic.
